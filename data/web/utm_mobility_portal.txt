UTM Mobility Portal

WHAT IS UTM MOBILITY PORTAL?

The UTM Mobility Portal is created to centralise all of the outbound mobility programmes promoted by UTM International to UTM students in a single page. This makes it easier for UTM student that is interested in joining the outbound mobility programmes to browse through the list of available and currently opened programmes.

HOW TO APPLY?

Any UTM student who wishes to undergo any of the outbound mobility programmes must submit the UTM outbound mobility application form. The student is required to print and submit the hardcopy version of the Study Abroad, Research Internship Abroad, International Invitation Program, Summer School Abroad or UTM Global Outreach Program application form.

The application form must be submitted along with the supporting documents to UTM International office before the deadline. UTM JB students can submit the form at S19 whereas UTM KL students can submit the form at Menara Razak.

Important Notes

If the host institution provides an application form for the program and it is appended in the UTM Mobility Portal, UTM student is also required to submit the form together with the UTM outbound mobility application to the UTM International office.

If the host institution provides an online application form, UTM student is required to fill it up after UTM International has successfully nominated the student's application to the host institution.
